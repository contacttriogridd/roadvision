How it works

A two-stage cascade: a fast CNN proposes regions, and a transformer checks each one. Fusion weight and thresholds were calibrated on the validation split only.

STEP 01

Ingest & validate

Type, magic-byte, size and resolution checks before anything touches the GPU.

STEP 02

Propose

YOLOv8s at 512 px emits low-threshold candidate boxes in milliseconds.

STEP 03

Verify

ViT-S/16 classifies each context-padded crop into 4 damage types plus background.

STEP 04

Fuse & score

Score = α·YOLO + (1−α)·ViT with α, then a severity index for maintenance priority.

Measured, not claimed

Held-out test split, IoU > 0.5 with matching label. Numbers are read live from this deployment's evaluation file.
